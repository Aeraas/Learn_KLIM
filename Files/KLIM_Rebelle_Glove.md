## What it is
The Rebelle Glove is KLIM's women's-specific off-road and dual-sport glove at $44.99. Built with women's hands in mind from the outset ("designed by women for women"), it uses AX® Suede Diez on the palm and fingers, a stretch nylon backhand, TPU knuckle protection, and a perforated 3mm XRD® foam palm pad — the palm XRD is a meaningful detail that the men's equivalent Dakar Glove does not include.

It covers enduro, dual-sport, and light adventure riding, prioritising fit, mobility, and grip over CE certification or leather construction.

## Who it's for
- Women riders looking for an off-road and dual-sport entry glove with a true women's-specific fit
- Women buying the Badlands, Carlsbad, or Dakar jacket who want a matched glove designed for off-road use
- Women who find men's or generic "fit-reduced" gloves uncomfortable — the Rebelle is a ground-up women's design, not a resized men's product
- **Not suited to** wet-weather riding (no membrane), road-only use where CE certification is required, or cold conditions. For a CE-certified waterproof women's glove, the Women's Adventure GTX Short (5027-001) is the right alternative.

## Common questions customers might ask + good answers

**"Is this just a smaller version of the Dakar Glove?"**
No — it's a genuinely different design built for women's hands. The palm and finger proportions differ from the men's Dakar Glove. It also includes a 3mm XRD foam palm pad that the men's Dakar Glove does not have. Same price, but the Rebelle has slightly better palm protection.

**"Is this CE certified?"**
No. TPU knuckle protection and XRD foam are present, but the glove is not certified to EN 13594. For CE-certified women's options, the Women's Adventure GTX Short Glove (5027-001) is waterproof and CE Level 1 KP. The Induction (5028-002) is CE Level 1 KP and available in XS–3X including women's smaller sizes.

**"What does AX® Suede Diez mean?"**
It's KLIM's branded synthetic suede — developed for off-road and MX use. "Diez" refers to the specific construction grade. It's more abrasion-resistant than basic synthetic materials, grippy in all conditions, and significantly more flexible than goat leather. Used in off-road gloves because it gives better feel and control on the bars and controls compared to stiffer leather.

**"What's TPU knuckle protection?"**
Thermoplastic Polyurethane — a harder plastic than the TPR (Thermoplastic Rubber) used in some off-road gloves. It gives a firm, protective knuckle shell that holds its shape. It's not as hard as carbon fibre or polycarbonate, but it's a solid protector for off-road impacts at the speeds and scenarios these gloves are designed for.
