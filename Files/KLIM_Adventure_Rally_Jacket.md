## What it is
The Adventure Rally is KLIM's **flagship adventure/rally overshell** — "the jacket that rewrote the rulebook on world-class ADV gear," now in its **sixth generation**. It's a 3-layer Gore-Tex over-armour shell built for the most demanding rides: Vectran™ fibre and variable-density SuperFabric® slide zones for extreme abrasion and cut resistance, a huge array of zippered vents, a neck-brace-compatible zip-off collar, and an integrated hydration system. It sits at the very top of KLIM's shell-over-padding philosophy — the premium sibling to the Raptor GTX Overshell, with rally-grade materials and features.

## Who it's for
- **Serious, well-funded adventure/rally riders** who run a KLIM Tactical Pro armour base layer and/or Ai-1 airbag
- Riders who want KLIM's most capable ADV overshell and top-tier abrasion materials (Vectran™ + SuperFabric®)
- Riders using a neck brace (the zip-off collar is neck-brace compatible)
- **Not suited to:** anyone expecting an all-in-one armoured jacket (no armour included); riders who won't wear a separate armour layer; budget-conscious buyers (this is a $1,200 shell before you add armour); road-only riders (the Latitude/Carlsbad are better value on tarmac).

## Common questions customers might ask + good answers

**"Does it come with armour?"**
No — it's an over-armour shell, built to go over a KLIM Tactical Pro armour base layer or an Ai-1 airbag vest. There are no pads in the jacket. Budget for the armour layer as part of the setup.

**"What's the CE rating?"**
KLIM don't publish an EN 17092 garment rating on the current page, so I won't quote you a figure I can't confirm. What I can tell you is the abrasion materials are top-tier — Vectran™ fibre and variable-density SuperFabric®, the same class of materials as their AAA-rated Badlands Pro A3. If a stated rating matters, let's confirm it in-store.

**"How is it different from the Raptor GTX Overshell?"**
Both are Gore-Tex over-armour shells with SuperFabric®. The Adventure Rally is the premium, rally-grade one — it adds Vectran™ fibre, far more ventilation (12 vents vs 6), a hydration system and a neck-brace-compatible collar, and it's roughly double the price. The Raptor is the more accessible off-road shell.

**"Is it worth it over the Carlsbad or Badlands?"**
Only if you want a modular armour/airbag shell system for serious ADV/rally riding. The Carlsbad and Badlands are complete armoured jackets and better value for most riders. The Adventure Rally is for the rider who specifically wants the top rally overshell and runs their own armour layer.

**"Which generation should I buy?"**
Both are the same core rally shell. The Gen 6 is current; the Gen 5 closeout is a big saving (around $700 off original) for essentially the same capability. If the closeout size and colour work for you, it's excellent value.
