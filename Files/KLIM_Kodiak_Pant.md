## What it is
The Kodiak Pant is the matched bottom half to KLIM's premium road-biased adventure touring jacket. Like the jacket, it's built around a Gore-Tex 3-layer Pro shell with perforated goat leather overlays on the knees — a combination that gives genuine abrasion protection without sacrificing the waterproof membrane. EU sizing applies to both gens.

The Gen 4 is described by KLIM as focused on three things: improved abrasion protection, more ergonomic construction, and a "massive upgrade in impact protection" with Rogue™ EXP Level 2 armour. Critically, it restores the CE AA rating lost on the Kodiak 2 due to the stretch crotch gusset. For road-touring customers — the Kodiak's primary audience — this is the headline change.

Motolegends (2025 range article) note the Kodiak pant's leg openings are too wide for typical European road boots. This is the same wide-leg adventure cut found on the Carlsbad and Badlands pants — designed for ADV boots and standing on pegs. Staff should set this expectation early.

## Who it's for
- **Buyers of the Kodiak Jacket** who want the full matched suit with jacket-pant zip connection
- Adventure touring riders primarily on tarmac who need a Gore-Tex 3-layer Pro pant with leather knee protection
- Riders using EU sizing — the Kodiak pant runs in EU waist sizes (48–64), unlike most KLIM products
- **Not ideal for** slim road boot wearers (leg opening is wide), lightweight-first buyers, or anyone who rarely encounters sustained rain

## Top selling points

1. **CE AA restored — the headline upgrade from Kodiak 2.** The Kodiak 2 pant shared the jacket's CE A rating due to the stretch crotch gusset. Gen 4 resolves this with a seamless crotch design, returning to CE AA. For a customer who previously looked at the Kodiak and walked away over the rating, that objection is now gone — on both the jacket and the pant.

2. **Gore-Tex 3-layer Pro + perforated goat leather on the knees.** The knees are the primary slide contact point for a pant. Most adventure pants at this level use fabric reinforcement (SuperFabric, Cordura). The Kodiak puts real goat leather there — a material choice that favours road-biased riding where extended slides are the main protection scenario.

3. **Matched EU sizing with the jacket.** The Kodiak uses EU waist sizing (48–64) — as does the jacket (48–64 chest). For European riders, this means both pieces are sized consistently, reducing the guesswork in getting a matched suit to fit correctly.

4. **Rogue™ EXP Level 2 hip and knee — included as standard.** Gen 4 ships with KLIM's newest, most breathable armour. No upgrade conversation needed.

5. **Suspenders included.** Unlike the Carlsbad pant where suspenders are a separate purchase recommendation, the Kodiak pant includes KLIM Deluxe Suspenders in the box — good to mention as it's often a question with adventure pants.

## Common questions customers might ask + good answers

**"Does the pant also have the CE A problem from the Kodiak 2 jacket?"**
Yes — the Kodiak 2 pant is also CE A, for the same type of reason. The stretch Gore-Tex crotch gusset (for mobility) lowered the rating to CE A just as the stretch panels under the jacket arms did. The crotch is a low-contact zone in most crashes, but the rating is still CE A. The Gen 4 pant has a seamless crotch design and is back to CE AA. If a customer is choosing between the two, the CE A situation applies to the whole Kodiak 2 suit.

**"Why is the leg so wide?"**
The Kodiak pant is designed for adventure touring — the wide leg accommodates large off-road boots, kneeguards, and allows full freedom when standing on pegs. On the road with slimmer boots, it can look baggy. This is a known Kodiak characteristic — Motolegends specifically mention it. Encourage customers to try the pant with their actual riding boots before buying.

**"What's the size note about compact knee armour?"**
Sizes 30 and smaller (in US terms) get a compact version of the Rogue™ EXP knee pad to keep the pant proportioned correctly on smaller legs. Sizes 32 and up get the full standard-size pad. The protection level is the same — it's a fit decision. Worth mentioning to customers in the smaller size range so they're not surprised.

**"The pant includes suspenders — does it still need them?"**
The pant has an adjustable elastic waist built in, so it functions without suspenders. The KLIM Deluxe Suspenders are included and recommended for all-day riding where the waist might shift, especially for riders with a smaller waist relative to hips. Encourage customers to try both on together.

**"Can I wear the Kodiak pant with a different jacket?"**
Yes — the jacket-pant zip is compatible with other KLIM jackets that have the connection zip. But the matched CE AA rating and EU sizing system is designed specifically for the Kodiak jacket. For a complete, matched, correctly-fitted CE AA suit from top to bottom, the Kodiak jacket is the natural pair.

**"What sizes are available in the Kodiak 2 closeout?"**
Regular: 48 through 60 (size 56 and 64 are gone). Short: 25–27 only. Tall: 106 and 114 only. Check in-store for exact colour availability — Cool Gray, Stealth Black, and Navy Blue are the known Kodiak 2 colours, all marked closeout.
