# KLIM Maverick Down Jacket

> ⚠️ **NOT a riding jacket.** The Maverick is a **mid-layer thermal garment** — no CE certification, no armour, no crash protection. It must be worn UNDER a riding jacket. Selling this as standalone riding protection is a safety risk. If a customer wants warmth and crash protection in one garment, direct them to the Marrakesh or Carlsbad + mid-layer system.
>
> 🧥 **Best sold as:** The thermal companion to the Marrakesh, Carlsbad, Kodiak, or any other KLIM jacket without a built-in liner. Also worth flagging: the Kodiak already **includes** the Maverick Down bundled in — check before suggesting an add-on purchase.

## What it is
The KLIM Maverick Down Jacket is a 800-fill-power goose down mid-layer jacket designed specifically to pair with KLIM's riding jackets. It's the insulation piece in KLIM's layering system. Originally bundled with the Kodiak 2 as its included thermal liner, it's now also sold separately — making it the premium upsell for any KLIM jacket customer who wants serious cold-weather riding warmth.

What makes it different from a generic high-street down jacket is the engineering for motorcycle use: seamless baffles (no stitch holes = no compressed areas, no cold spots, and critically no velcro snagging on jacket liners), a slim profile for under-jacket wear, DWR water-repellent treatment, 3M Scotchlite reflective tabs, and the ability to pack into its own pocket with no separate stuff sack.

Motolegends (2021 Battle of the Downs comparison): *"a simply brilliant goose-down jacket — we think it's the business."* They compared it directly against the Rukka Down-X and Belstaff Albion jacket, and all three were warmer than typical mid-layer liners. Their Maverick recommendation comes up across multiple product reviews — it's their go-to thermal pairing for Marrakesh and Carlsbad customers.

## Key features

- **800-fill-power goose down** — 90/10 goose down/feather blend, RDS (Responsible Down Standard) certified gray goose down
- **Seamless baffles** — no stitch holes through the shell: zero compressed cold spots, zero snagging against a jacket's inner velcro or lining
- **Windproof 20D nylon outer shell** — blocks windchill, extremely lightweight
- **DWR (Durable Water Repellent) treatment** — light water resistance; not fully waterproof (wear under a waterproof jacket in rain)
- **Packs into own pocket** — no separate stuff sack required; packs small; great for the back box when the sun comes out
- **Elastic cuff binding** — seals cuffs against wind and heat loss, designed to sit under riding jacket sleeves
- **In-pocket hem adjustment** — tighten the hem to seal heat in without a separate adjustment cord
- **2 zip hand pockets**
- **3M Scotchlite™ reflective tabs** — visible even when worn under a jacket in case visibility matters at a stop
- **YKK® zippers**
- Colours: Insignia Blue, Castlerock, Stealth Black (current); Petrol (closeout)
- Sizes: XS–3X

## Top selling points

1. **800-fill-power is serious insulation.** Consumer down is often rated 550–650 fill. 800-fill is the upper end of premium insulation — warmer per gram than most alternatives, which means the Maverick can be genuinely thin and still significantly warm.

2. **Seamless baffles = no cold spots, no snagging.** Standard down jackets stitch through the baffles, creating thin, cold lines and catching on velcro inside jackets. The Maverick is engineered specifically to avoid both — a real differentiator vs generic alternatives.

3. **Packs into its own pocket.** For touring riders who only need the Maverick for colder stretches of a journey, this is key — it folds down small enough to live in a jacket pocket or top box, and there's no stuff sack to lose.

4. **Dual purpose: layer and standalone jacket.** The Maverick is designed to look like a premium casual jacket off the bike. Riders travelling internationally can wear it at the destination without looking like they're in motorcycle gear. Motolegends call this the "destination jacket" appeal.

5. **The Marrakesh + Maverick + Zephyr system.** Motolegends' 2025 range article outlines this explicitly as the ideal KLIM layering system: Zephyr Wind Shirt (base) + Maverick Down (mid) + Marrakesh (outer) = "one jacket and three little bundles, good to go anywhere." This is a strong upsell package for a customer buying the Marrakesh.

## Common questions customers might ask + good answers

**"Can I wear this instead of a riding jacket?"**
No — the Maverick has no armour and no CE certification. It must be worn inside/under a proper riding jacket. It's insulation, not protection.

**"Why spend $275 on a mid-layer when I can buy a cheap down jacket?"**
Three things: fill power (800 vs typically 550 in budget alternatives = warmer per gram), seamless baffles (no cold spots, no snagging on velcro inside jackets — a problem every rider who's used a supermarket down jacket under their riding jacket has experienced), and motorcycle-specific design (elastic cuffs, DWR treatment, slim under-jacket profile, packs into pocket). If they're going to spend serious money on a KLIM jacket, skimping on the thermal layer is a false economy.

**"Is 800 fill power that different to, say, 650?"**
Fill power measures how much loft (and thus insulation) you get per ounce of down. At 800 fill, you get roughly 20-25% more warmth per gram than 650-fill down, meaning the same jacket can be made lighter OR warmer. At the temperature extremes most touring riders face, that difference is meaningful.

**"What colours is it available in?"**
Current: Insignia Blue, Castlerock, Stealth Black. Petrol is available as closeout stock — confirm in-store.

**"The Kodiak says it includes the Maverick — is this the same jacket?"**
Yes — the bundled thermal liner in the Kodiak is the KLIM Maverick Down Jacket, the same product sold separately here. If a customer buys the Kodiak, they already own a Maverick and don't need to purchase one separately.
