## What it is
The Badlands Aero Pro Short is KLIM's hot-weather adventure riding glove — and arguably the most ventilated leather motorcycle glove in the KLIM range. It's built around two ventilation systems working together: mapped big-bore perforations across both the palm and backhand, and moulded finger air intake scoops — ducts on three fingers that actively channel air into the glove interior during forward motion. No mesh panels, no insulation, no waterproofing. Pure airflow.

But it's not a lightweight compromise glove. The knuckle protection is dual-material: carbon fibre and polycarbonate combined, backed with 5mm XRD® foam. The palm carries 5mm XRD foam. The finger stitching is outseam — external seams, the same comfort-focused construction as the Induction Glove, the most refined approach to finger seaming in the KLIM lineup. The shell is premium goat leather throughout.

Motolegends describe it as *"about as vented as a glove gets, yet it's something you'd be prepared to wear on the road at proper riding speeds."* That captures the positioning: this is a serious leather adventure glove that happens to be engineered for summer.

CE Level 1 KP certified, EN 13594:2015.

## Who it's for

## Key features

- **Shell:** Premium goat leather — both palm and backhand; structured and durable for road abrasion resistance
- **Ventilation system 1 — mapped big-bore perforations:** Large-diameter perforations mapped across the entire glove — both sides of the fingers, the palm surface, and the wrist — engineered to place holes where abrasion risk is lower while preserving intact leather at impact zones
- **Ventilation system 2 — finger air intake scoops:** Moulded ducts on three fingers; physically channel airflow into the interior of the glove during forward motion; a dynamic ventilation feature rather than passive perforation alone
- **Knuckle protection:** Carbon fibre + polycarbonate dual-material knuckle guard + 5mm XRD® foam — hard dual-shell outer absorbs impact energy; XRD beneath provides non-Newtonian foam energy absorption; ceramic print overlay for abrasion resistance
- **Palm protection:** 5mm XRD® Impact Protection Foam — full palm area coverage; ceramic print overlay
- **Finger stitching:** Outseam construction — seams are stitched to the exterior of the finger; the interior is smooth leather against the finger; no internal seam ridges; the most refined and comfortable glove construction; identical to the Induction Glove's approach
- **Low-profile palm seams:** Palm seams are minimised to reduce hot spots under grip pressure
- **Finger knuckle pads:** Vented TPU — rigid, perforated pads at each finger knuckle; add protection and allow airflow at the dorsal finger surface
- **Wrist cuff protector:** TPU + 2mm foam wrist cuff — structural protection at the wrist cuff edge
- **Wrist closure:** VELCRO® strap with plastic clip connector — standard secure closure
- **Visor wiper:** Yes — index finger
- **Touchscreen:** MULT-E-TOUCH™ with precision pads
- **Reflective:** 3M™ Scotchlite Carbon Black reflective detailing
- **CE:** Level 1 KP, EN 13594:2015 — certified knuckle protection
- **Colours (3924-003 current):** Stealth Black, Rifle Green–Oil Green, Monument Gray, Black–Hi-Vis
- **Colours (3924-001 closeout):** Stealth Black, Peyote–Potter's Clay, Petrol–Strike Orange, Monument Gray

No other KLIM glove combines both passive perforation and active ducted ventilation. 2. **Outseam finger stitching — the gold standard in glove comfort.** The same construction as the Induction Glove: all finger seams are stitched externally, leaving the interior smooth leather directly against the finger. For a customer who already knows the Induction Glove's comfort, the Badlands Aero Pro carries the same feel in a vented adventure format. For anyone who hasn't tried outseam — put them in a glove with it and they'll feel the difference immediately.

3. **Dual-material hard-shell knuckle: carbon + polycarbonate.** Not a soft rubber knuckle, not just polycarbonate alone. The carbon fibre + polycarbonate combination provides a rigid, high-strength outer shell. At adventure riding speeds, a hard-shell knuckle guard is a meaningful safety upgrade over a softer TPR or rubber protector. XRD foam beneath adds energy-dispersal protection.

4. **5mm XRD in both the knuckle and the palm.** Double coverage with impact-absorbing foam at the two primary hand contact zones in a forward fall. The 5mm depth provides meaningful energy absorption without adding noticeable bulk. XRD's non-Newtonian behaviour means it's compliant in normal use and stiffens on impact.

5. **CE Level 1 KP certification — certified, not just claimed.** The knuckle protection is tested to EN 13594:2015 KP Level 1. For a customer comparing to non-certified summer gloves, this is a concrete differentiator.

6. **Black–Hi-Vis (current gen only).** The 3924-003 Black–Hi-Vis colourway is a high-visibility option that pairs with safety-conscious kit choices. Only available on the current gen; the closeout doesn't have this option.

## Common questions customers might ask + good answers

**"What are the finger scoops actually for?"**
They're moulded ducts on three of the fingers — shaped to capture air at the leading edge of the finger and channel it into the interior of the glove. At motorway speeds, they actively pump air through the perforations rather than just relying on passive airflow through holes. The result is meaningfully more airflow than perforated leather alone delivers. It's an active ventilation system built into the glove structure.

**"How does this compare to the Induction Glove?"**
Both use outseam finger stitching and goat leather — the comfort baseline is the same. The Badlands Aero Pro is more vented (big-bore perforations across both sides, plus finger air scoops) and has a harder dual-material carbon + polycarbonate knuckle guard. The Induction uses a carbon knuckle with XRD but without the additional polycarbonate shell. The Badlands Aero Pro is the adventure-spec variant; the Induction is the road/touring variant at $149.99 vs $179.99 (current gen).

**"Is there a waterproof version of this glove?"**
Not directly — the Badlands GTX Long ($269.99) is the all-weather insulated Badlands glove, but it's a gauntlet with Thinsulate, not a vented short glove. There is no Badlands Aero Pro GTX. If a customer needs waterproofing, the recommendation is to carry a pair of waterproof overmitts or switch to the Vanguard GTX Short or Adventure GTX Short for wet conditions.

**"What's the difference between the two gens in stock?"**
The 3924-001 (closeout) is $20 cheaper at $159.99 but only comes in SM, MD, 2X, and 3X — LG and XL are not available in that SKU. The 3924-003 (current) is $179.99 and has the full size run XS–3X, plus updated colourways including Black–Hi-Vis. The protection specs are the same. If the customer's size is SM or MD, both are available; if they need LG or XL, only the current gen works.

**"Is the knuckle really carbon fibre?"**
Yes — it's a dual-material guard using both carbon fibre and polycarbonate. Carbon fibre provides high strength-to-weight structural stiffness; polycarbonate provides impact toughness. Combining the two gives a knuckle that's both rigid and resistant to fracture on impact. It's backed with 5mm XRD foam for energy absorption. CE Level 1 KP certified.
