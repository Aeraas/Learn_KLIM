## What it is
The Badlands Pro A3 is KLIM's highest-rated, most protective road jacket — and arguably the most protective Gore-Tex motorcycle jacket ever made. The "A3" designation refers to its CE **AAA** rating under EN 17092-2, the highest level in the European motorcycle clothing standard. As of its Gen 2 launch, it was the first Gore-Tex garment in history to achieve CE AAA certification.

What makes the A3 different from the standard Badlands Pro isn't just the armour grade — it's the **Vectran™ Fiber** used throughout the shell. Vectran is a liquid-crystal polymer with higher strength and cut resistance than even SuperFabric. Combined with SuperFabric on impact zones, Rogue™ Level 2 armour on shoulders/elbows/back, and full Rogue™ Level 1 chest pads (all included), this is a jacket built to protect at the absolute limit of what consumer motorcycle apparel can offer.

Motolegends are candid: they acknowledge the A3 is exceptional, but note it shares the Badlands family characteristic of being large, heavy, and boxy — better suited to expedition riders than everyday users.

## Who it's for
- **Riders who want the maximum possible protection** — CE AAA is the highest rating achievable
- Long-distance expedition adventure riders who prioritise protection above all other factors

- Customers who ask specifically "what's the safest jacket you sell?" — this is the answer

## Common questions customers might ask + good answers

**"What's CE AAA and why does it matter?"**
The European safety standard EN 17092 grades motorcycle clothing on how well it protects in a slide. AA (like our Carlsbad and standard Badlands Pro) is the second highest level. AAA is the top level — it means the jacket passes significantly more rigorous abrasion and impact tests. The A3 is the only jacket we sell at AAA.

**"What's Vectran™? Why does it matter?"**
Vectran is a high-performance liquid-crystal polymer fibre used in aerospace, deep-sea rope, and cut-resistant gloves. In the A3 shell, it replaces the standard textile found in the Badlands Pro. It's more resistant to cuts and abrasion than even SuperFabric. Think of it as the jacket's "frame" being made from a fundamentally tougher material.

**"Is the A3 worth the premium over the standard Badlands Pro?"**
If CE AAA and maximum abrasion resistance are priorities, yes — these are genuine, measurable differences. If the customer's primary concern is waterproofing, versatility, or comfort, the standard Badlands Pro or even the Carlsbad may serve them better at lower cost. The A3 is a specialist tool.

**"How does this compare to the Artemis?"**
The Artemis (Gen 3) is CE AA on a 2-layer Gore-Tex shell with SuperFabric — excellent and notably more wearable day-to-day. The A3 adds a full tier of CE rating (AAA vs AA), Vectran fibre, and a 3-layer Gore-Tex shell. The Artemis is for riders who want premium everyday protection; the A3 is for those who want the absolute maximum.
