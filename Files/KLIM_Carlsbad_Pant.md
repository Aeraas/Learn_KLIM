## What it is
The Carlsbad Pant is the matching bottom half to KLIM's most popular adventure suit. It follows the same "everything you need, nothing you don't" ethos as the jacket — Gore-Tex Performance Shell (2-layer), CE AA rated, Level 2 armour at hip and knee, goat leather on the inside of the knees for tank grip, and a wide-leg adventure cut that fits over off-road boots.

The Gen 4 is a meaningful refresh: Rogue™ Level 2 hip and knee armour replaces the older D3O LP2 Pro, and for the first time the Carlsbad Pant is available in Short and Tall lengths alongside Regular — a significant fitment improvement that makes it accessible to more riders.

Motolegends are candid about the wide leg cut: it's designed for adventure/off-road boots and standing on pegs. On slimmer touring boots it can look baggy. Staff should be prepared to manage this expectation and encourage customers to try them with the right footwear before buying.

## Who it's for
- **Buyers of the Carlsbad Jacket** who want the full CE AA rated suit — the jacket-pant connection zip locks both together for complete waterproofing at the waist
- Adventure and dual-sport riders who need a CE AA waterproof pant that fits over off-road boots
- Riders who prioritise mobility and standing on the pegs (wider cut facilitates this)
- **Not ideal for** slim-leg tourers or riders with smaller adventure/road boots who find the wide leg cut excessive

## Common questions customers might ask + good answers

**"Do I need to buy the pant to get a fully rated suit?"**
Technically you can buy the jacket alone and it's CE AA certified. But the pant is also CE AA and shares the same Gore-Tex shell — so pairing them gives you a complete, head-to-toe rated waterproof adventure suit. The jacket-pant zip also seals the waist gap, which matters for serious rain riding.

**"Why do the legs look so wide?"**
The Carlsbad is designed for the adventure/off-road market — wider legs accommodate off-road boots, kneeguards, and allow full movement when standing on pegs. If your customer is primarily road riding with slimmer boots, be honest: it's a looser cut than a road-specific pant. For that style of riding, the Latitude jacket paired with a more road-focused pant might be better.

**"What's the difference between the Gen 3 and Gen 4 armour?"**
Gen 4 has Rogue™ Level 2 — KLIM's newest system, lighter and more breathable than the D3O LP2 Pro in the Gen 3. Both are Level 2 (the highest), but Rogue gets more air to the body. For someone who runs hot or does longer days in the saddle, Gen 4 is the comfort upgrade. Gen 3 is still excellent protection for $260 less.

**"What sizes are available?"**
Gen 4 is the first Carlsbad pant to offer Short (for shorter inseam) and Tall options, alongside Regular. Waist sizes run 30–42 in Regular and Short, 32–38 in Tall. If they've had fitment issues with adventure pants before, this is worth flagging.

**"Does it come with braces/suspenders?"**
No — braces are sold separately. The pant has two quick-adjust waist straps built in, but KLIM braces are an optional add-on worth mentioning for customers who ride all day or who find the waist a little loose with just straps.
