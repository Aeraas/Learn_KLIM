> 1. **No armour is included.** The Sixxer ships with empty armour pockets — D3O CE Level 1/2 (or Rogue™) armour is **sold separately**. A customer riding away in it as-supplied has **no impact protection**.
> 2. **No EN 17092 CE rating is published** on the KLIM product page, and KLIM positions it as a **casual** leather jacket. Do not present it as a certified-abrasion motorcycle jacket — confirm any CE claim in-store.

## What it is
The Sixxer is KLIM's **premium leather** jacket — the only leather in the range and a completely different category from their textile/Gore-Tex line. It's a classic-styled 0.9–1.1mm premium cowhide jacket with modern touches: comfort-mapped perforation for airflow, magnetic collar retention, and a mesh comfort liner. KLIM describe it as "a classic motorcycle jacket" with "intelligent functionality" — a supple, casual, ride-ready leather jacket rather than a hardcore armoured touring piece.

## Who it's for
- Riders wanting a **classic leather look** — cruiser, retro, roadster, café styling
- Riders who prefer leather's feel and abrasion character and will **add their own armour**
- Customers after a KLIM leather jacket that works on and off the bike
- **Not suited to:** buyers who want armour included and ready to ride (it isn't); anyone wanting a stated CE abrasion rating (not published); waterproofing or serious touring/adventure use (this is leather, not Gore-Tex — see Carlsbad/Latitude); riders who won't fit armour themselves (guide them to fit the D3O pads before riding).

## Common questions customers might ask + good answers

**"Does it come with armour?"**
No — it has adjustable elbow pockets and a back-pad pocket, but the pads themselves (D3O or Rogue™ Level 1/2) are sold separately. I'd fit armour before riding in it; happy to sort the right pads for you now.

**"What's its CE rating?"**
KLIM don't publish an EN 17092 garment rating for the Sixxer, and they class it as a casual leather jacket, so I can't quote you a certified figure. Leather has its own abrasion character, but if a published CE rating is what you're after, our textile options like the Carlsbad are the ones that carry a stated AA rating.

**"Is it waterproof?"**
No — it's leather, not a Gore-Tex jacket. It'll handle a light shower but it's not a wet-weather touring jacket. For waterproofing, look at the Latitude or Carlsbad.

**"Can I wear it off the bike?"**
Very much so — that's part of the point. Classic styling, supple leather and a comfortable liner make it wear well casually, then armour up for the ride.

**"How does the perforation affect warmth?"**
It's designed for airflow, so it's cooler than solid leather in summer — great in warm weather, but you'll want a layer underneath when it's cold.
