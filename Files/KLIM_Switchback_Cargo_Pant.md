# KLIM Switchback Cargo Pant

> 🔶 **No waterproof membrane.** The Switchback is a single-layer, unlined, breathable riding pant designed for warm-weather and off-road use. It has DWR treatment for light showers but will not keep a rider dry in sustained rain. This is by design, not a shortcoming.
>
> ⚠️ **D3O Ghost armour orientation:** The knee and hip armour pads have **two sides — the orange side faces toward the rider's body, the black fabric side faces outward**. KLIM specifically flag this on the product page. Staff should brief customers on fitting.
>
> 🧥 **No jacket-pant zip connection** — the Switchback is a standalone pant, not part of a paired suit system. It pairs well with the Marrakesh jacket or any adventure jacket.

## What it is
The Switchback Cargo Pant is KLIM's adventure/dual-sport riding pant for riders who want proper motorcycle protection in a pant that looks and feels like a quality cargo trouser — not like a technical riding suit. It's built from a breathable stretch woven chassis with Cordura® canvas overlays at the knee, seat, and cuff, CE AA rated (EN 17092-3), and loaded with seven pockets in a wide, loose adventure cut.

This is the 3917-001 version — the redesign. The previous model (3917-000) was CE A; the current version achieved CE AA. Motolegends' initial 2023 order was told the Switchback had failed to pass EN 17092 entirely; it came back with a CE AA rating — a significant upgrade they were "truly excited" about.

Motolegends' summary: *"The Switchback is a fabulous trouser. We don't have anything else quite like it; and for some people it will be the perfect riding pant."*

## Who it's for
- Adventure and dual-sport riders who want a CE AA pant that doesn't look like motorcycle gear
- Riders doing hot-weather or off-road riding where breathability matters more than waterproofing
- Customers pairing with the Marrakesh jacket or any non-waterproof adventure jacket — a natural match
- Riders who want a large waistband size range with three inseam lengths (30/32/34)
- **Not suited to** sustained rain riding, commuting in smart clothing, or anyone who expects a jean or chino look — these are clearly an adventure/off-road trouser

## Key features

**Shell & Construction**
- **Multipurpose cargo-style riding pant** — breathable stretch woven chassis; this is the pant's defining quality: it stretches, breathes, and moves
- **Stretch Cordura® canvas overlays** on knee, seat, and cuff — durable reinforcement at wear zones without stiffening the whole pant
- **DWR (Durable Water Repellent) treatment** — light water shedding; not waterproof, not membrane-lined
- **3M™ Scotchlite™ Reflective Material** + hidden reflective panel inside bottom cuffs (pull tab reveals it)
- **CE AA rated** to EN 17092-3 — upgraded from CE A on the previous model; a genuine protection improvement

**Armour**
- **D3O® IP Ghost CE Level 1 vented adjustable knee armour** — slim, low-profile, inserted via a flap beneath the cargo pocket from the outside
- **D3O® IP Ghost CE Level 1 vented hip armour**
- **Armour can be removed while wearing the pant** — no need to take them off
- ⚠️ **Ghost armour has two sides:** orange toward the body, black fabric outward. Fitting it backwards compromises its function.

**Ventilation**
- **Breathable stretch woven chassis** provides passive air permeability throughout — not just at vent openings
- **2 zippered thigh vents** along the sides of the cargo pockets — the primary active vents; Motolegends rate these highly
- **2 calf double-headed entry zippers** — also function as vents; Motolegends note these are shorter than on the old model and less effective as vents when worn over taller boots

**Storage — 7 pockets**
- 2 hand pockets
- 1 zippered thigh cargo pocket with internal organiser
- 1 double-snap thigh cargo pocket
- 2 seat pockets (1 zippered)
- 1 thigh knife pocket

**Fit & Comfort**
- **Streamlined interior — no liner** — wears directly against skin or base layer; maximises breathability
- **Articulated patterning + stretch material** — excellent mobility; designed for standing on pegs and moving off-road
- **Fits with common street motorcycle boots** — not just tall off-road boots; the leg opening accommodates regular road boots too
- **Belt loops** — wears like a normal trouser at the waist
- **Snap adjust bottom hem** — elasticated strap with poppers clamps the leg around the boot. Motolegends: *"so clever we're surprised that nobody has copied it"*
- **US waist x inseam sizing** — sizes run 30–40 waist in inseam 30, 32, and 34 options (see size chart for full matrix)
- Colours: Teak, Monument Gray, Black/Asphalt

## Top selling points

1. **CE AA on a cargo pant.** The Switchback achieves CE AA (EN 17092-3) — the same rating as the Carlsbad and Badlands Pro pants — in a pant that looks like an outdoor/adventure cargo trouser. For customers who want CE AA protection without a technical motorcycle appearance, this is a rare option.

2. **Breathability over everything.** No membrane, stretch woven chassis, 2 zippered thigh vents, and passive airflow throughout. For hot-weather or sustained off-road riding, the Switchback will be significantly more comfortable than any Gore-Tex adventure pant. The Carlsbad will be cooler than the Badlands but it still has a membrane. The Switchback doesn't.

3. **Seven pockets.** Hand pockets, two cargo pockets on the thighs (one zippered with organiser, one snap), two seat pockets, and a knife pocket. For adventure riders who carry tools, navigation devices, snacks, or documentation across their body, this storage layout is genuinely useful.

4. **Three inseam lengths.** The size matrix runs 30–40 waist with inseam options of 30, 32, and 34 (availability varies by waist size — confirm in-store). This is the same three-length approach as the KLIM Gore-Tex pants, and unusual for a cargo-style riding trouser.

5. **The boot hem adjuster.** The elasticated strap with poppers at the hem is a KLIM design detail Motolegends specifically highlight — it clamps the leg cleanly around any boot without zip-on gaiters, velcro, or complicated closures. Easy to use, effective, and distinctive.

## Common questions customers might ask + good answers

**"Is this waterproof?"**
No — the Switchback has DWR coating to shed light rain briefly, but no waterproof membrane. This is intentional: a membrane would trap heat and block airflow, which defeats the purpose of this pant. For riding in sustained rain, customers should be in the Carlsbad or Badlands pant instead. The Switchback is at its best in warm, dry conditions.

**"What CE rating does it have?"**
CE AA, certified to EN 17092-3 — the same rating as the Carlsbad and Badlands Pro pants. The previous Switchback was CE A; this model was redesigned to achieve AA. For a cargo-style single-layer pant, CE AA is a strong result.

**"What's the D3O Ghost armour and how does it fit?"**
D3O Ghost is D3O's thinnest, most flexible armour format — it sits very flat against the body and is almost invisible under the pant. It's CE Level 1 rated. The knee pad inserts from the outside of the pant via a flap under the cargo pocket — not through the leg. Important: the orange side of the pad goes toward the rider's body; the black fabric side faces outward. Fitting it backwards is a common mistake.

**"Why no liner?"**
The Switchback is designed for breathability-first. A liner would trap heat against the body and slow airflow through the stretch woven chassis. Without a liner, air flows directly through the fabric, keeping the rider cooler in hot conditions. For cooler days, riders can add base layer leggings underneath.

**"What jacket does it pair with?"**
There's no jacket-pant connection zip on the Switchback — it's a standalone pant. Motolegends recommend it with the Marrakesh jacket for a versatile adventure look that works both on and off road. It'll also pair with the Dakar jacket, any of the adventure jackets, or any non-paired jacket the customer already owns.

**"How does sizing work?"**
The Switchback uses US waist x inseam sizing (e.g. 34x32), not EU sizing. Waist sizes run from 30 to 40, with inseam options of 30, 32, or 34 inches — though not every combination is available in every colour. Check the size chart and in-store stock before promising a specific combination.
