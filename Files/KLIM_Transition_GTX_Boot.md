## What it is
The Transition GTX is KLIM's waterproof lifestyle boot — designed for riders who want a single pair of footwear for commuting, errands, and the ride itself, in a low-profile package. It uses Gore-Tex waterproofing in a leather upper with a BOA Fit System, mid-height shaft, NESTFIT anatomically optimised footbed, and a rugged outsole. At $229.99 (SKU 3094-002), it sits between the Blak Jak GTX ($259.99) and Blak Jak non-GTX ($219.99) on price.

However, the Transition GTX is categorised differently from the Blak Jak and Outlander. KLIM describe it as footwear to "get you to and from the ride" — it is positioned as lifestyle footwear that happens to be waterproof, not as a motorcycle boot with certified impact protection. There is no CE certification to EN 13634:2017 listed, no XRD or impact foam padding, and no dedicated motorcycle-specific construction.

For customers who want proper CE-certified motorcycle footwear, the Blak Jak or Outlander GTX are the correct products. The Transition GTX suits a different customer: someone who wants a waterproof everyday boot that can handle the commute and doesn't want to carry separate footwear.

## Who it's for
- Commuters who want one versatile waterproof boot for riding to work, walking to the office, and running errands — prioritising lifestyle versatility over motorcycle-specific protection standards
- Riders who live in wet climates and want a waterproof boot with good all-day walking comfort for mixed on/off-bike days
- Customers who are conscious about not overpaying for motorcycle features they don't need — e.g., someone riding a scooter or small-displacement commuter in an urban environment
- **Not suited to** customers who want CE-certified motorcycle impact protection; customers doing adventure, off-road, or touring riding; or anyone who has been told they need motorcycle boots to meet insurer or employer requirements (always verify — the Transition does not list motorcycle CE certification)

## Common questions customers might ask + good answers

**"Is this a proper motorcycle boot?"**
It's a waterproof leather boot designed for riders — for commuting, daily use, and getting to and from the ride. It does not list CE certification to EN 13634:2017, which is the standard for motorcycle boots. For CE-certified options, the Blak Jak (from $219.99) and the Outlander GTX ($299.99) are the right products. If the customer specifically needs a CE-certified motorcycle boot, direct them there.

**"How does this compare to the Outlander GTX at similar money?"**
Different design intent. The Outlander GTX ($299.99) is a dedicated short adventure motorcycle boot — CE certified, 5mm XRD ankle pucks, polycarbonate lasting board, off-road sole with peg cutouts, built to be ridden and walked in on trails. The Transition is a waterproof everyday boot without motorcycle protection certification. For serious motorcycle use, the Outlander is the right choice. For a customer who just wants Gore-Tex waterproofing in an everyday boot they also ride in, the Transition is $70 cheaper.

**"Why does the Transition have BOA but the more expensive Blak Jak GTX doesn't?"**
Different design philosophies. The Blak Jak uses traditional metal lace eyelets for a classic work-boot aesthetic. The Transition is more contemporary in design and uses BOA for ease of entry. The BOA on the Transition is the same quality system as the Outlander, with the lifetime guarantee. If fast on/off is a priority, the Transition (or Outlander) has an advantage over the Blak Jak.
