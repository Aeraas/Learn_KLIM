# KLIM Induction Pro Jacket

> **⚡ Not the same jacket as the standard Induction.** The **Induction Pro** (SKU 5056-001, **$549.99**) is the higher-spec sibling: a mix of Karbonite™ mesh and Marrakesh-style stretch Cordura, perforated goat leather overlays, and **full Level 2 D3O armour** (shoulder, elbow *and* back). The standard **Induction** ($449.99) is a full open-mesh jacket with **Level 1** armour. Both CE AA. See [Induction vs Induction Pro](#induction-vs-induction-pro-vs-marrakesh) below.

## What it is
The Induction Pro is KLIM's flagship hot-weather road jacket — engineered for class-leading airflow in genuinely extreme heat, without dropping to a budget mesh spec. It combines an industrial-grade Karbonite™ nylon mesh chassis with the same stretchy 1000D Cordura used on the Marrakesh, perforated goat leather on the elbows and shoulders, and comes fully kitted with Level 2 D3O armour at every point including the back. It's CE AA rated. Motolegends' verdict: *"we really rate the Induction Pro jacket"* — but with a clear caveat that it's built for the American Sunbelt and can flow *too much* air for typical UK riding.

## Who it's for
- Riders heading somewhere genuinely hot — **southern Europe, Morocco, Australia, India**, or US Sunbelt states
- Riders who want **maximum airflow with Level 2 protection built in** (no upgrades needed)
- Road riders who found the standard Induction's spec a step short and want the premium chassis and goat leather overlays
- **Suit buyers** — pairs with the Induction Pant (note: the matching pant is the standard Induction Pant, Level 1 armour — there is no "Pro" pant)
- **Not suited to:** everyday UK riding as a sole jacket (flows so much air you'll get cold fast — layer aggressively or choose the Marrakesh); serious off-road (that's the Baja S4, boxier fit); buyers who want the comfiest all-rounder (Marrakesh is more comfortable and better for layering); tight budgets (the standard Induction is $100 less).

## Top selling points

1. **Class-leading airflow with Level 2 armour as standard.** This is the pitch. It flows more air than almost anything KLIM makes, yet ships with full Level 2 D3O at shoulder, elbow *and* back. Most jackets make you trade airflow for protection or pay extra for Level 2 — the Pro gives both out of the box.

2. **A premium chassis, not a budget mesh.** Marrakesh-grade 1000D stretch Cordura in the stretch zones, 600D Cordura overlays, and **perforated goat leather on the elbows and shoulders** — the same premium overlay approach as the Kodiak. It breathes like a mesh jacket but is built like a proper KLIM.

3. **Level 2 protection all round, including abrasion benefit.** Level 2 armour delivers not just more impact protection but added abrasion resistance too. Combined with the AA-rated shell, this is a seriously protective summer jacket.

4. **brrr° cooling liner + 360° reflectivity.** Chemical-free cooling tech in the sleeves adds to the on-bike comfort, and full 360-degree reflectivity is a real safety plus for a hot-weather jacket people wear on long summer days and into the evening.

5. **The honest UK conversation builds trust.** Motolegends are candid that this jacket can flow *too much* air for the UK — steer the customer to layer (Zephyr + Maverick) or, if they want one everyday jacket, to the Marrakesh. Leading with that honesty is a stronger sale than overselling the Pro to a UK commuter.

## Common questions customers might ask + good answers

**"What's the difference between the Induction Pro and the standard Induction?"**
Both are CE AA hot-weather mesh jackets. The Pro ($549.99) uses a premium chassis — Marrakesh-grade stretch Cordura, 600D overlays and perforated goat leather on the elbows and shoulders — and comes with full **Level 2** D3O armour (including the back). The standard Induction ($449.99) is a simpler open-mesh build with 500D overlays and **Level 1** armour. Pro = more protection and a more premium build; standard = more airflow-per-pound at a lower price.

**"How does it compare to the Marrakesh?"**
The Pro flows more air and comes with Level 2 armour as standard; the Marrakesh is more comfortable, stretchier, better for layering, and more understated. For most UK riders wanting one jacket, Motolegends still lean Marrakesh. For riders who specifically want maximum airflow and built-in Level 2 protection — especially for hot-climate travel — the Pro is the pick.

**"Is it any good for UK riding?"**
Only on genuinely warm days as a standalone — it's built for desert-grade heat and flows so much air you'll get cold quickly when the temperature drops. The fix is layering: a Zephyr Wind Shirt under it, a Maverick Down for cooler days, and a waterproof over the top when it rains. If they want one jacket for typical British weather without fuss, point them at the Marrakesh.

**"Is it waterproof?"**
No — it's a mesh jacket, designed to flow air, so it's the opposite of waterproof. Pair it with a waterproof over-layer, or look at a Gore-Tex laminated jacket (Carlsbad / Latitude) if staying dry is the priority.

**"What armour does it come with?"**
Full **Level 2** D3O at the shoulders, elbows and back — all included, all removable, elbow armour adjustable. You can upgrade to KLIM's Rogue™ Level 2 if preferred, but you don't need to add anything for Level 2 protection.

**"Is there a matching pant?"**
Yes — the Induction Pant ($449.99). Important to flag: it's the standard Induction pant, **not** a Pro version, so it comes with Level 1 armour rather than Level 2. Off-road riders wanting a wider pant for big boots should look at the Baja instead.

**"Pro or Baja S4?"**
Same airflow class, but the Pro is a road jacket (closer fit, Level 2 armour) and the Baja is off-road (boxier, roomier, but only Level 1 armour). Motolegends prefer the Pro for most riders because of the better armour. Choose the Baja only if the off-road fit and the wider matching pants matter.
