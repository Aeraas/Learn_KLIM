## Notes for web display

### Women's specific
- **Rebelle** is a women's-specific glove (different sizing, fit, and palm proportions — not a unisex product in a smaller size)
- The **Adventure GTX Short** also has a women's variant (SKU 5027-001 Women's vs 5031-002 men's) — stock may vary in store

### CE rating clarification
- KP = Knuckle Protection under EN 13594:2015
- KP1 = Level 1 (the only KP level in the current KLIM glove range — no KP2 products stocked)
- **Dakar** and **Rebelle** carry no CE certification — staff must not present these as certified motorcycle gloves

### Waterproof membrane note
- All waterproof gloves in this range use **Gore-Tex + Gore Grip**: the membrane is bonded directly to the leather outer (laminate construction), not floating as a loose inner liner. This means the glove won't wet out in extended rain — a key difference from cheaper waterproof construction

### Vanguard GTX Long knuckle / gasket
- File does not confirm knuckle type or neoprene gasket for the Vanguard GTX Long — staff should verify in store. Neoprene gasket is confirmed on the **Badlands GTX Long** only

### Outseam stitching
- Confirmed outseam construction: **Induction**, **Badlands Aero Pro Short**
- Lap-seam (not outseam): **Marrakesh**
- Reduced seams (not full outseam): **Dakar Pro**
- Unconfirmed / not stated: Adventure GTX Short, Vanguard GTX Short, Vanguard GTX Long, Badlands GTX Long

### Visor wiper distinction
- **Dual wiper (Badlands GTX Long only):** Left index = water wiper · Right index = ice scraper — no other KLIM glove in range has this
- **Dakar Pro:** Goggle squeegee on thumb — for off-road / goggle use, not a visor wiper
- **Rebelle:** Sweat wipe on index finger — off-road equivalent of a visor wiper
