## What it is
The Badlands Pro Pant is the matching bottom to KLIM's flagship expedition adventure jacket. Like the jacket, it's purpose-built for long-distance, multi-week adventure travel in harsh conditions: Gore-Tex 3-layer Pro shell, SuperFabric on the knees, full Level 2 armour at hip and knee, 6 vents, leather inside the knees, and enough detail to match the jacket's expedition-grade specification.

The Gen 5 upgrade centres on the armour — Rogue™ EXP Level 2 replaces D3O Aero Pro Level 2, delivering the same protection in a lighter, more breathable package. Motolegends' editorial stance mirrors their view on the jacket: the Badlands is for committed world travellers, not everyday adventure riders. Staff should frame it accordingly.

## Who it's for
- Buyers of the Badlands Pro Jacket who want the full matched CE AA suit
- Long-distance expedition riders doing sustained, multi-week touring in demanding conditions
- Customers who want Gore-Tex 3-layer Pro at the trouser level (the Carlsbad pant uses 2-layer)
- **Not suited to commuting, casual touring, or riders who want a slim-fitting trouser** — the Badlands pant fits baggy and large, designed for maximum mobility and off-road boot clearance

## Top selling points

1. **Gore-Tex 3-layer Pro at trouser level.** Most adventure pants — including the Carlsbad — use 2-layer Gore-Tex. The Badlands Pro pant uses the same 3-layer Pro shell as the jacket, making this a genuinely matched expedition suit from top to bottom in durability terms.

2. **SuperFabric on the knees.** In a slide, the knees are the primary contact point for trousers. SuperFabric's ceramic print overlay is the highest abrasion resistance material KLIM uses in their pant line — positioned exactly where it's needed most.

3. **6 vents including lower leg.** The Carlsbad pant has 4 vents. The Badlands adds 2 lower leg vents for additional airflow on sustained hot-weather riding. Small detail, meaningful on a long day in warm conditions.

4. **Rogue™ EXP armour at hip and knee, included.** Same story as the jacket — Gen 5 ships with KLIM's newest armour as standard. Lighter, more breathable, expanded coverage vs D3O.

5. **Full short and tall sizing.** The Gen 5 offers Regular (28–42), Short (28–40), and Tall (32–40) — a meaningful range for riders who've historically found adventure pants the wrong length. Worth proactively mentioning to any customer who looks like they might need a non-standard length.

## Common questions customers might ask + good answers

**"What's the difference between this and the Carlsbad pant?"**
Two key things: shell and armour. The Badlands pant uses 3-layer Gore-Tex Pro (more durable, stiffer) vs the Carlsbad's 2-layer. The Badlands also has SuperFabric on the knees, an extra 2 vents (6 vs 4), and the full Badlands expedition construction. It's also significantly more expensive. For most riders, the Carlsbad pant is a perfectly capable alternative at lower cost.

**"Do I need the Badlands pant to match the Badlands jacket?"**
Not technically — any KLIM pant with the jacket-pant zip will connect. But for a CE AA rated suit from top to bottom, you want a CE AA rated pant. The Carlsbad pant is also CE AA if the customer wants a lighter, more comfortable alternative at lower cost. The Badlands pant gives the complete 3-layer Pro + SuperFabric system that mirrors the jacket spec.

**"Why does the size note say compact vs standard knee armour?"**
Size 30 and smaller get a compact (smaller footprint) knee armour to keep the pant's profile correct on smaller legs. Size 32 and up get the standard-size Rogue™ EXP knee pad. Protection level is the same — it's a fit/proportion decision. Staff should mention this for customers in size 30 or below so there's no surprise at fitting.

**"What sizes are available?"**
Gen 5 runs Regular 28–42, Short 28–40, and Tall 32–40. Gen 4 closeout: sizes 36 and 38 only — check stock before offering.
