# KLIM Zephyr Wind Shirt

> ⚠️ **This is NOT a primary riding jacket.** It has no CE certification and no armour. It is a windproof layering piece worn UNDER a mesh or breathable jacket. Never sell it as a standalone riding garment — but it is an outstanding upsell alongside the Marrakesh, Dakar, or any non-waterproof riding jacket.

## What it is
An ultra-thin, packable windproof layer designed to make mesh and breathable jackets wearable in cooler conditions. Built from 4-way stretch fabric with a laminated windproof/water-resistant membrane, it packs down to the size of a tennis ball and weighs almost nothing. Motolegends call it "the best return on investment in motorcycle apparel."

## Who it's for
- **Any rider wearing a mesh or vented jacket** (Marrakesh, Dakar, etc.) who rides in variable weather — the Zephyr extends the usable temperature range of any breathable jacket
- Riders who want some protection against showers and wind chill without carrying a full waterproof jacket
- Adventure and touring riders who want extra warmth under a waterproof jacket without adding bulk
- Works off the bike too — customers wear it dog walking, golfing, commuting under a suit jacket

---

## Why wind chill matters — the key talking point for staff

Motolegends explain it well: riding at 70 mph in 6°C ambient temperature, your body feels -2°C due to wind chill. A mesh jacket like the Marrakesh flows air through every panel — exactly what makes it brilliant in the heat — but that same airflow becomes punishing when the temperature drops or the sun disappears. The Zephyr's membrane blocks the wind without trapping heat, solving this problem. Same science as a Gore-Tex layer, but at a fraction of the weight and price.

## Common questions customers might ask + good answers

**"Can I wear this as my riding jacket?"**
No — it has no armour, no CE certification, and no abrasion resistance. It must always be worn under a proper riding jacket. If someone wants a light windproof riding jacket as their primary jacket, that's a different product category entirely.

**"Is it waterproof?"**
Not fully — the membrane isn't taped at the seams, so rain will eventually work through. Motolegends estimate about an hour's protection in a shower. It's a solid "get you home" option, but not a substitute for a proper waterproof layer like the Carlsbad or Altitude. Think of it as very good wind protection with a bonus shower buffer.

**"How much warmer does it actually make you?"**
It doesn't add insulation — it blocks wind chill. But blocking wind chill is very effective: at 70 mph in 6°C, it can turn a perceived -2°C back to near the actual ambient temperature. For genuinely cold riding they still want a thermal layer underneath; the Zephyr handles the wind, not the cold itself.

**"Will it fit under my jacket?"**
Almost certainly — it's gossamer-thin and 4-way stretch. Fits under mesh jackets, waterproof jackets, leather. Customers can try it on over their current kit in the store.

**"Is there a women's version?"**
