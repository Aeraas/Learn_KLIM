## What it is
The Marrakesh Glove is KLIM's dedicated warm-weather street riding glove — a second-generation redesign built around goat leather with stretch woven panels, rubber knuckle protection backed with XRD® foam, and a lap-seam finger construction designed to maximise breathability and comfort. No waterproofing, no insulation. CE Level 1 KP certified.

KLIM describe it as "KLIM's most comfortable summer riding glove" — the emphasis is on all-day wearability in hot conditions, making it the natural companion to the Marrakesh Jacket (KLIM's best-selling mesh adventure jacket).

Compared to the Induction Glove ($149.99), the Marrakesh is more budget-accessible ($99.99), uses a rubber rather than carbon-fibre knuckle protector, and uses lap-seam rather than outseam finger construction. The Induction's outseam stitching is the defining comfort differentiator at that higher price point; the Marrakesh's lap-seam is still a quality construction, just not external-seam.

## Who it's for
- Riders who already own the Marrakesh Jacket and want a glove from the same system
- Hot-weather street and adventure riders who want CE Level 1 KP protection without leather stiffness
- Customers who want a step up from the Dakar Pro in terms of road-focused construction (leather, CE certified) at a lower price than the Induction
- **Not suited to** wet riding (no waterproofing), cold conditions, or riders who specifically want the outseam comfort construction of the Induction

4. **Lycra fourchettes for lateral airflow.** The breathable material between the fingers is not just leather — it's a high-breathability Lycra panel. In a hot-weather glove, the inter-finger zone is a primary heat exit point. This is a thoughtful detail.

5. **CE Level 1 KP at a summer glove price.** At $99.99, certification to EN 13594:2015 KP Level 1 is included as standard. The Marrakesh isn't a compromise product — it's a properly CE-certified leather glove at summer glove money.

## Common questions customers might ask + good answers

**"How does this compare to the Induction Glove?"**
Both are goat leather, CE Level 1 KP, hot-weather gloves without waterproofing. The Induction has a carbon fibre hard-shell knuckle protector (over XRD foam) and outseam finger stitching — the most comfortable construction in the range. The Marrakesh has a rubber knuckle protector (over XRD foam) and lap-seam construction. The Induction is $50 more. If a customer tries both on, the Induction's outseam construction typically wins on feel; the Marrakesh is the choice if the budget is a constraint or the customer primarily wants a Marrakesh Jacket companion.

**"Is this waterproof?"**
No — the Marrakesh Glove has no waterproof membrane. It's designed for dry, warm conditions. For waterproofing, the Vanguard GTX Short or Adventure GTX Short are the alternatives. If a customer is building a Marrakesh Jacket kit, they should carry a separate pair of waterproof gloves for rain.

**"What's lap-seam construction?"**
The finger seams are folded flat and stitched in a lapped position, rather than turned inside-out (internal seam — uncomfortable ridge against the finger) or turned to the outside (outseam — Induction's approach, the most comfortable). Lap-seam is between the two — no sharp internal ridge, but not as refined as full outseam. A step up from most standard leather gloves.

**"Is this only available in black?"**
Based on the current KLIM product page, black is the only listed colour for the 3718-001. Check in-store for any additional stock.
