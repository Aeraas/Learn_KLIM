## Vanguard GTX Short (3922-001)

### What it is
The Vanguard GTX Short is KLIM's touring glove for the rider who wants full-time waterproofing and doesn't want to carry separate wet-weather gloves. KLIM's key positioning: "temperature neutral" — no venting holes, no insulation, designed to perform across a range of cool-to-warm riding conditions without committing to either end. It uses goat leather, a soft rubber and XRD foam knuckle protector (no hard carbon shell), and a dual Velcro+YKK zip cuff entry system. CE Level 1 KP certified.

Motolegends stock it in black. They describe the knuckle protection as "a D3O-like soft protector with XRD foam backing" — a compact, unobtrusive protector that doesn't bulk out the glove.

### Key features (Short)

- **Shell:** Goat leather palm and fingers
- **Waterproof:** Gore-Tex + Gore Grip laminate — membrane bonded to outer leather; will not wet out
- **Knuckle:** Semi-pliable rubber + foam knuckle protector + embossed leather overlay + 5mm XRD® impact foam beneath — a soft/flexible outer rather than a hard carbon shell
- **Palm:** 5mm XRD® impact protection foam; embossed leather overlay
- **Finger/thumb knuckle pads:** 3mm comfort foam
- **Cuff:** Short — designed for under-jacket-cuff use; Velcro + YKK® zip dual-entry (zip makes getting in/out easy in wet conditions)
- **Inner cuff glide panel:** Helps entry when hands or glove interior are wet
- **Liner:** Moisture-wicking
- **Visor wiper:** Yes (index finger)
- **Touchscreen:** MULT-E-TOUCH™ with precision pads
- **Reflective:** 3M™ Scotchlite™ Carbon Black
- **Entry assist pull loop:** Yes
- **CE:** Level 1 KP, EN 13594:2015
- **Colours:** Stealth Black, Monument Gray
- **Sizes:** XS, SM, MD, LG, XL, 2X, 3X

## Top selling points (both models)

1. **Full-time Gore-Tex laminated waterproofing.** The membrane is bonded to the outer leather — it won't wet out in sustained rain or cold water. Both models carry KLIM's "Guaranteed To Keep You Dry™" promise.

2. **Goat leather touring construction.** Both models use goat leather — durability, abrasion resistance, and feel across multiple seasons. This is a touring glove for consistent riders, not an occasional-use piece.

3. **CE Level 1 KP certified.** Both models are certified to EN 13594:2015 KP Level 1 — the knuckle protection standard for motorcycle gloves. Relevant when customers compare to uncertified winter gloves.

4. **Short = temperature versatility; Long = cold-specific performance.** The Short's "temperature neutral" design means one glove for most of the riding season; the Long's Thinsulate makes it a committed cold-weather tool that does the job properly when temperatures are low.

5. **Sizes XS–3X (Short).** Full size range on the Short — worth mentioning for customers who struggle to find gloves at the extremes.

## Common questions customers might ask + good answers

**"How is this different from the Adventure GTX Short?"**
Both are short-cuff, waterproof, Gore-Tex laminated leather gloves. The Vanguard Short uses a soft rubber knuckle protector (more comfortable, less obtrusive, but not a hard shell); the Adventure GTX Short uses a carbon fibre hard knuckle over XRD foam. The Vanguard Short's dual Velcro+zip entry system is a practical differentiator in wet conditions. KLIM positions the Vanguard as a touring glove (road-focused); the Adventure GTX as an ADV glove (road and off-road).

**"What does 'temperature neutral' mean?"**
No insulation, no ventilation holes. The glove performs across a range of temperatures without committing to either warmth or cooling. Roughly: 8°C–22°C is a reasonable range (depending on rider sensitivity). Below that, the Vanguard Long's Thinsulate makes more sense. Above it, an unlined leather glove like the Adventure GTX Short will be cooler.

**"Is the Long much warmer than the Short?"**
Meaningfully warmer. The 60g Thinsulate on the backhand is the main difference — it's a proper insulation layer, not just a liner. The Long is suited to sub-10°C riding; the Short is not. If a customer rides in winter, the Long is the answer.

**"What's the dual-entry cuff on the Short?"**
There's a Velcro flap and a YKK® zip. The zip unzips the cuff aperture wider — particularly useful when trying to get gloves on and off in the rain with a wet jacket or wet hands. The inner cuff glide panel also reduces friction on entry.
