# KLIM Krios Pro Helmet

> **⚡ Positioning (read first):** The Krios Pro is KLIM's original carbon KOROYD® adventure helmet — now the **more off-road-leaning, lighter, lower-priced sibling** to the newer [X1 Alpha](KLIM_X1_Alpha_Helmet.md). Since the X1 launched, the Krios Pro has effectively become the *dirt-biased* choice. For road-primary adventure-touring, steer most riders to the X1; for genuinely off-road-heavy riders who want the lightest lid, the Krios Pro.
>
> **⚡ Certification:** Unlike the X1 page, the Krios Pro page **does** state its standard: **ECE 22.06 / DOT certified.** You can quote that with confidence.
>
> **⚡ Price note:** KLIM's product page shows **$724.99** (SKU 3610-001). Cross-sell tiles across the site show inconsistent figures ($599.99 in places) — quote **$724.99** unless confirmed otherwise. Some colourways are on **closeout** (cheaper). UK — confirm in-store.

## What it is
The Krios Pro was the **first ADV-specific DOT helmet in North America to use KOROYD®** impact-absorbing technology, and it's still a genuinely trick, feather-light carbon adventure helmet. Hand-laid full-carbon shell, KOROYD® liner, four ride modes, a Fidlock® chin strap and a photochromic visor plus Pinlock in the box. It's the helmet the X1 Alpha was designed to succeed — and while the X1 is the better road/touring all-rounder, the Krios Pro remains a strong pick for riders who genuinely spend their time off the tarmac and prize low weight.

## Who it's for
- **Off-road-heavy adventure riders** who want the lightest KLIM lid and don't mind more noise/wind for it
- Riders who love the Fidlock® chin strap and KLIM's off-road styling
- Buyers wanting a carbon ADV helmet with photochromic visor + Pinlock included, at a lower price than the X1
- Riders after a closeout colour bargain
- **Not suited to:** road-primary touring riders (the X1 Alpha is quieter, seals better in rain, and fits more head shapes — recommend it instead); riders sensitive to wind noise (Motolegends flag the Krios as notably noisy); anyone who needs the plushest cheek pads or best wet-weather visor seal (both are X1 improvements).

Lead with the X1 for typical UK/European ADV riders; offer the Krios Pro to weight-obsessed or genuinely off-road-focused buyers, or as a value option on closeout colours.

## Top selling points

1. **Lightest carbon ADV helmet in the KLIM line.** 1450 g (L), and around 1350 g in a Medium — noticeably lighter than the X1 and well under the class benchmark. For weight-focused off-road riders, this is the headline.

2. **Pioneering KOROYD® protection.** The first ADV DOT helmet in North America to adopt KOROYD® — the honeycomb tubes crush uniformly to absorb impact and aid airflow. A genuine technical story with real heritage.

3. **ECE 22.06 / DOT — the current standard, clearly stated.** Unlike some listings, the Krios Pro page prints the exact certification, so you can reassure customers it meets the current ECE 22.06 requirement.

4. **Loaded box + Fidlock strap.** Photochromic visor and Pinlock included (the photochromic alone is a ~£200 add-on on a Shoei), plus the clever Fidlock magnetic chin strap many riders love — and which the X1 actually dropped.

5. **Now the value/dirt option.** With the X1 above it, the Krios Pro is the lower-priced, lighter, more off-road-focused choice — and closeout colours make it a genuine bargain for the right rider.

## Common questions customers might ask + good answers

**"Krios Pro or X1 Alpha?"**
For most riders — especially road and touring-biased — the X1 Alpha is the better helmet: quieter, cooler, better rain seal, and it fits more head shapes (3 shells / 5 EPS sizes). Choose the Krios Pro if you want the lightest lid, genuinely ride off-road a lot, love the Fidlock strap, or want to save money on a closeout colour.

**"What's it certified to?"**
ECE 22.06 and DOT — the current ECE standard. It's printed clearly on the spec, so no ambiguity here.

**"Is it noisy?"**
Honestly, yes — it's the Krios Pro's known weak spot. Its KOROYD® liner transmits shell noise more than a conventional helmet, and the visor seal isn't the best in heavy rain. If quietness matters to you, the X1 Alpha specifically fixes both. If you mostly ride off-road at lower speeds, it's less of an issue.

**"How heavy is it?"**
About 1450 g in Large, roughly 1350 g in Medium — very light for a carbon adventure helmet, and lighter than the X1. That's a real plus on long off-road days.

**"Does it come with a sun visor?"**
No drop-down sun visor, but you get a photochromic face shield in the box that darkens automatically, plus a clear shield and a Pinlock insert.

**"Can I fit a comms system?"**
Yes — it's SENA 10U compatible, which is a dedicated integration for this helmet. Worth noting the older comms experience wasn't perfect, so set expectations, but the 10U is the intended unit.

**"Why are some colours cheaper?"**
Those are closeout (non-current) colourways — same helmet, discounted because the colour is being run out. A good way to get the Krios Pro at a lower price if the colour works for you.
