# KLIM Baja Jacket (Gen 3) & Baja S4 (Gen 2)

> **⚠️ Naming gotcha — read this first.** KLIM currently lists **two** Baja jackets, and the names are counter-intuitive:
> - **"Baja Jacket"** (SKU 4061-002, **$659.99**) = the **newer Gen 3**, upgraded to KLIM **Rogue™ Level 1** armour.
> - **"Baja S4 Jacket"** (SKU 4061-001, **$599.99**) = the **older Gen 2**, on **D3O® Level 1** armour.
>
> The "S4" badge does **not** mean newer or higher-spec — it's the previous generation. Check the SKU and armour type, not the name. Both are CE AA and share the same chassis concept. See [Gen 2 vs Gen 3](#gen-2-baja-s4-vs-gen-3-baja--at-a-glance).

## What it is
The Baja is KLIM's dedicated **off-road** hot-weather mesh jacket — built for the hottest riding on Earth: open deserts, sand dunes, humid jungle. It uses an industrial-grade breathable mesh chassis with 4-way stretch panels for unrestricted airflow and mobility, ceramic-plated **SuperFabric®** on the shoulders and elbows for abrasion resistance, and comes CE AA rated. It's designed as a kit: pair it with a waterproof shell (KLIM's Enduro S4) over the top and it becomes a genuinely all-weather adventure setup. Sits alongside the road-focused Induction Pro as the two highest-airflow jackets KLIM makes.

## Who it's for
- **Serious off-road and adventure riders** in hot, dry or humid climates
- Riders who want KLIM's **most airflow** and an off-road-appropriate, roomier fit for standing on the pegs
- ADV riders building a layering kit (Baja + waterproof shell + insulating mid-layers)
- Riders who want **SuperFabric® abrasion protection** in a mesh jacket
- **Not suited to:** UK/temperate everyday road riding as a sole jacket — it flows so much air you'll get cold fast (layer, or choose the Marrakesh/Induction Pro); riders wanting a close road fit (the Baja is deliberately boxier/baggier — the Induction Pro is the road-cut equivalent); anyone wanting Level 2 armour as standard (both Bajas ship Level 1 — upgrade separately).

1. **KLIM's most airflow, built for real heat.** Born for deserts, dunes and jungle — full-time chassis ventilation through industrial mesh. If a customer says "I ride somewhere seriously hot and want the coolest jacket you've got," this and the Induction Pro are the two to show.

2. **SuperFabric® abrasion protection in a mesh jacket.** Ceramic-plated SuperFabric® on the shoulders and elbows is the highest abrasion-resistant material in the KLIM range — normally seen on premium adventure suits, here on the highest-airflow jacket. Big reassurance for a mesh buyer.

## Common questions customers might ask + good answers

**"What's the difference between the 'Baja' and the 'Baja S4'?"**
They're two generations of the same jacket, and the naming is confusing. The plain **Baja** (4061-002, $659.99) is the newer Gen 3 with KLIM's Rogue™ Level 1 armour. The **Baja S4** (4061-001, $599.99) is the older Gen 2 with D3O Level 1 armour. Both are CE AA with SuperFabric® overlays. The "S4" isn't a higher spec — check the SKU and armour, not the name.

**"Is it good for UK riding?"**
Only on genuinely hot days as a standalone — it's built for desert-grade heat and flows more air than almost anything, so you'll get cold quickly when temperatures drop. It comes into its own layered up under a waterproof shell, or for hot-climate travel. For everyday British riding, the Marrakesh is the easier one-jacket answer.

**"Baja or Induction Pro?"**
Same airflow class. The **Baja is off-road** — roomier fit, wider matching pants for big boots, and (Gen 3) Rogue Level 1 armour. The **Induction Pro is road** — closer fit and Level 2 armour as standard. Motolegends actually prefer the Induction Pro for most riders because of its Level 2 armour. Choose the Baja if the off-road fit and boot-friendly pants matter to you.

**"What armour does it come with, and should I upgrade?"**
Both gens ship Level 1 (Gen 3 = Rogue™, Gen 2 = D3O), shoulder/elbow/back included. Motolegends' honest take: for the hard off-road riding this jacket is built for, they'd upgrade to Level 2 (Rogue™ Level 2, or a body-armour vest). Worth mentioning to anyone buying it for serious dirt use.

**"Is it waterproof?"**
No — it's a mesh jacket, designed to flow air. It has a DWR finish that sheds light water briefly, but for real rain you layer a waterproof shell (Enduro S4) over the top. That over-shell approach actually gives better waterproofing than a built-in membrane.

**"What's SuperFabric®?"**
A ceramic-print abrasion overlay — the highest abrasion-resistance material KLIM uses, normally reserved for their top adventure suits. On the Baja it's on the shoulders and elbows, the areas that hit the ground first. It's a real protection edge for a mesh jacket.

**"Can I make it a full kit?"**
Yes — it zips to the matching Baja S4 Pant ($519.99), has a Baja S4 Glove ($99.99), routes a hydration bladder, and layers under the Enduro S4 waterproof shell. Just flag that the off-road pants are wide for big boots and are less ideal for pure road use.
